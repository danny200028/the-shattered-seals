# The Shattered Seals v1.1.0

Minecraft Java 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1.238 · Java 21

- The Warden's Sanctuary is a three-storey castle with a signed Grand Stair,
  a vaulted keep and 15 functional rooms spread across all three floors.
- The Trophy Hall, secret arena and vanilla spawn ward remain available.
  The new castle blueprint applies to future Keystone placements.
- Controlling adds search to **Options → Controls → Key Binds**. Searchables
  is included as its required library. Mouse Tweaks adds inventory shortcuts,
  and AppleSkin shows food and saturation information.
- The six-chapter campaign includes four classes, Shattered Depths, New Game+,
  repeatable bounties, Shattered Trials and legendary sword mastery.

Download `The-Shattered-Seals-1.1.0-CurseForge.zip` and import it as a Minecraft
modpack in CurseForge. The standalone JAR is also available. Back up existing
worlds before updating. The update preserves placed Sanctuaries and their
contents. New world generation appears in fresh or unexplored chunks.
See the [player guide](https://github.com/danny200028/the-shattered-seals/blob/v1.1.0/README.md)
for controls, progression and troubleshooting.
